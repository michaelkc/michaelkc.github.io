Tool

Clipboard image to description

Paste an image to generate a text description, locally in your browser.

Input

Click here, then press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V) with an image on your clipboard, or drop an image file.

PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, AVIF or BMP / up to 50 MB

Processing

Images and prompts stay in your browser. Model files download from Hugging Face and are cached when possible. Descriptions may be inaccurate.

Output

Ready

Paste an image to begin. The model loads on first use.
